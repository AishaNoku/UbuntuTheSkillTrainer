Lesson 2: Tools & Materials

Beginner-Friendly Materials, Yarn Types & Hook Sizes

Overview

In this lesson, you will learn about the essential tools and materials every beginner crocheter needs. Choosing the right tools helps you learn faster and avoid frustration.

Beginner-Friendly Tools

  • Crochet Hooks: Start with 5mm or 6mm.
  • Yarn: Medium weight (Size 4) acrylic is best for beginners.
  • Scissors: Small and sharp for cutting yarn.
  • Yarn Needle: Used for weaving in ends.
  • Stitch Markers: Helps you keep track of stitches.

Yarn Types

Here are the most common yarn categories for beginners:

  • Acrylic Yarn: Cheap, durable, perfect for beginners.
  • Cotton Yarn: Great for dishcloths & summer clothing.
  • Wool Yarn: Warm and stretchy, but harder to use at first.

Pro Tip: Start with acrylic yarn size 4 and a 5mm hook — it’s the easiest combination for learning!

Hook Sizes & How They Work

Hooks come in many sizes. The hook size determines how big your stitches will be.

Hook Size Best Yarn Type Difficulty
4mm Lightweight (Size 3) Intermediate
5mm Medium Weight (Size 4) Beginner Friendly
6mm Chunky (Size 5) Very Easy
