Lesson 4: Double Crochet (DC)

The double crochet is taller than the HDC and great for fast, airy projects.

What is a Double Crochet?

Double Crochet (DC) is commonly used for:

  • Scarves
  • Shawls
  • Blankets
  • Open patterns

How to Make a Double Crochet

  1. Yarn over before inserting your hook.
  2. Insert the hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop (3 loops on hook).
  4. Yarn over, pull through 2 loops.
  5. Yarn over again, pull through remaining 2 loops.
