Practice Patterns

Apply the stitches you learned in Module 2

Video Guide:

Pattern 1: Simple Chain + Single Crochet Row

This pattern helps you practice making an even foundation chain and working single crochet rows.

Instructions:

  • Make 10 chains.
  • Turn your work.
  • Single crochet across the chain (10 stitches).
  • Repeat for 5 rows.

Pattern 2: Single Crochet + Half Double Crochet

This pattern helps you transition between stitch heights smoothly.

Instructions:

  • Chain 12.
  • Row 1: Single crochet across the chain.
  • Row 2: Half double crochet across the row.
  • Repeat Rows 1 and 2 three times.

Pattern 3: Double Crochet Practice Square

Perfect for practicing even tension and stitch sizing.

Instructions:

  • Chain 15.
  • Row 1: Double crochet across.
  • Work 7 more rows of double crochet.
  • You should have a small square.

Pattern 4: Treble Crochet Stripes

Practice tall stitches while keeping edges straight.

Instructions:

  • Chain 20.
  • Rows 1–2: Single crochet.
  • Rows 3–4: Treble crochet.
  • Repeat twice.
