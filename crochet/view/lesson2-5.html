Lesson 5: Treble Crochet (TR)

The treble crochet is one of the tallest basic stitches, creating long and open textures.

What is a Treble Crochet?

Treble Crochet (TR) is ideal for:

  • Lightweight shawls
  • Lacy scarves
  • Decorative edges
  • Fast-growing patterns

How to Make a Treble Crochet

  1. Yarn over twice before inserting your hook.
  2. Insert hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop (4 loops on hook).
  4. Yarn over, pull through 2 loops.
  5. Repeat until all loops are gone.
