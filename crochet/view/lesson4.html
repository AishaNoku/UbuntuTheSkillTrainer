Lesson 4: Making a Slip Knot

The First Step to Beginning Any Crochet Project

What Is a Slip Knot?

The slip knot is the foundation of almost every crochet project. It creates the first loop that attaches your yarn to the hook, allowing you to begin forming chains and stitches.

Steps to Make a Slip Knot

  1. Hold the yarn tail in your left hand and cross it over the working yarn to make a loop.
  2. Insert your fingers into the loop and pull the working yarn through it.
  3. Hold the tail and working yarn, then tighten the loop gently.
  4. Place the loop onto your crochet hook and pull the working yarn to snug it up.

Video Demonstration

Tip: The loop on the hook should be snug but still able to slide easily.

Common Mistakes

  • Making the knot too tight
  • Using the yarn tail instead of the working yarn
  • Pulling the loop unevenly
