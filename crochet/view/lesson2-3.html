Lesson 3: Half Double Crochet (HDC)

The HDC stitch is taller than a single crochet but shorter than a double crochet.

What is a Half Double Crochet?

The Half Double Crochet (HDC) is a versatile stitch used for:

  • Blankets
  • Clothing
  • Textured patterns
  • Fast-growing projects

How to Make a Half Double Crochet

  1. Yarn over before inserting your hook.
  2. Insert the hook into the stitch.
  3. Yarn over and pull up a loop (3 loops on hook).
  4. Yarn over and pull through all 3 loops.
