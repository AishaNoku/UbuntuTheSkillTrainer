Option 1: Crochet Scrunchie

Materials needed: Hair elastic, yarn (medium weight), 4mm hook.

  1. Attach your yarn to the hair elastic with a knot.
  2. Work single crochets all around the elastic until full.
  3. Add more stitches (up to 3 in each) to create a ruffled texture.
  4. Slip stitch to finish and weave in ends.

Option 2: Beginner Scarf

Materials needed: Yarn (thick/soft), 6mm hook.

  1. Chain 20–25 depending on desired width.
  2. Use single, half-double, or double crochet for each row.
  3. Crochet until the scarf reaches your preferred length.
  4. Add fringe for decoration (optional).
Sample Scarf

Option 3: Decorated Slippers

Materials needed: Slippers, small amount of yarn, 3–4mm hook.

  1. Crochet small flowers or borders (like scallops).
  2. Attach them carefully to the slippers using a needle or slip stitches.
  3. Reinforce any loose edges for durability.
