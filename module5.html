Intermediate Techniques

Welcome to the Intermediate Patterns module! In this lesson, you will learn three fundamental techniques:

  • Magic Ring: A technique used to begin crochet circles with no hole in the center.
  • Stitching in the Round: Working continuously in spirals or joined rounds.
  • Color Changes: Switching yarns smoothly for multi-color designs.
