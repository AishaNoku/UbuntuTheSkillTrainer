Module 4: Beginner Projects

Create fun and simple crochet items to practice your skills!

Project 1: Crochet Bracelet

What You'll Learn:

  • Working with chains
  • Making slip stitches
  • Adding decorative finishes

Steps:

  1. Chain 35–45 depending on wrist size.
  2. Slip stitch back across the chain.
  3. Tie or bead the ends for closure.

Project 2: Easy Coaster

What You'll Learn:

  • Working in the round
  • Making even increases
  • Creating smooth borders

Steps:

  1. Create a magic circle.
  2. Round 1: 6 sc in the circle.
  3. Round 2: 2 sc in each stitch.
  4. Round 3: *1 sc, inc* repeated.
  5. Continue until desired size is reached.

Project 3: Simple Headband

What You'll Learn:

  • Foundation chains
  • Even row stitching
  • Joining edges neatly

Steps:

  1. Chain 60–70 depending on head size.
  2. Work 5–7 rows of half double crochet.
  3. Slip stitch or sew the ends together.

Project 4: Mini Scarf

What You'll Learn:

  • Alternating stitches for texture
  • Neat edges
  • Basic strip construction

Steps:

  1. Chain 80.
  2. Row 1: Double crochet across.
  3. Row 2: Half double crochet across.
  4. Repeat Rows 1 & 2 until desired length.
