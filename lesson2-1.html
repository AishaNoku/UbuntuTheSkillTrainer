Lesson 1: Slip Stitch (SL ST)

The slip stitch is one of the simplest crochet stitches and is often used for joining rounds or creating smooth edges.

What is a Slip Stitch?

A slip stitch (SL ST) is the shortest and tightest crochet stitch. It helps you move across stitches without adding height and is perfect for:

  • Joining rounds in circular crochet
  • Creating flat seams
  • Finishing edges neatly
  • Moving the yarn to another position in your work

How to Make a Slip Stitch

  1. Insert your hook into the stitch you want to slip into.
  2. Yarn over (wrap the yarn over your hook).
  3. Pull through the stitch AND the loop on your hook at the same time.
  4. You have made a slip stitch!
